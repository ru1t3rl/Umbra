# @umbra/shell-dashboard

Shell (page composition, wiring only, ADR 0078): the live map page at `/` (M5b map; M8 adds the controls, the entity list, the log and the inspector, WI-14).

- `route` = `{ path: "/", label: "Live map", icon: "map", nav: true, lazy }`; the app registers it in `src/router.tsx`. `DashboardPage` is the default export of `src/components/DashboardPage/DashboardPage.tsx` and is code-split (it carries MapLibre).
- Layout: a floating glass `CommandBar` (AppNav, run status, `RunControls`, a Clock button with `ClockControl` in a Panel, a Scenario button, theme switch); a left glass panel `aside "Entities"` (Tabs Entities and Log, collapsible, collapsed at a viewport of 1280 px or less); a right panel `aside "Inspector"` while an entity is selected; the scenario loader in a tonal Card in the centre while the run is `idle` (a Panel opened by the bar's Scenario button otherwise); `ConnectionAlert` and `RunFaultAlert` under the bar. Never glass on glass.
- Wiring: one `useState<number | null>` selection goes to `LiveMap`, `EntityListPanel` and `EntityInspector`; `mapRef.centerOn` for "Center on map"; `useAffiliationOf()` and `useApplySubscription` (stream rate) from settings; recent scenario paths from settings to `ScenarioLoader`, whose `onLoaded` writes them back. The page never reads the run status: `ScenarioButton`, `ScenarioCentre` and `ScenarioPanel` subscribe on their own, so a frame does not re-render the map element.
- Bar: one row at every width. `useBarFit` measures the rendered bar (ResizeObserver on the bar and its content, at most once per frame, with hysteresis; pure rule in `model/fitLevel.ts`) and takes up to two steps: 1 drops the title and makes Clock, Scenario and the theme switch icon-only, 2 also makes the lifecycle buttons icons with `aria-label` and `title`. The lifecycle row never wraps. `RunControls layout="bar"` holds the lifecycle row; the Clock button opens a panel with `ClockControl` and `StepControls`; a failed command (lifecycle or step, one `CommandProblemProvider` slot) renders in the alert column. The Clock panel hangs from the bar's right edge. At most one of the Clock and Scenario popovers is open; each focuses its panel on open, closes on Escape and returns focus to its button. The Scenario panel is the last child of the alert column.
- Landmarks: the bar sits directly in the body so it stays the page `banner`; `<main>` holds a visually hidden `h1`, the map region, the loader and the alerts; the panels are `complementary` landmarks outside `main`.
- Insets: `useBarInset` measures the rendered bar (margins plus the ui-kit `--vt-cmdbar-height`) and passes `insets={{ top }}` to `LiveMap` and `--dashboard-inset-top` to the page, so the panels and the alerts stay clear of the bar (ASM-508).
- `src/testing.tsx` holds the test doubles (a map stub, a fake socket, a fake `fetch`, `mountDashboard`); it is not exported.
